## 🤔 FAQ

**Q: Is this real money?**  
A: No, it's testnet. All Pi/TriSyn are test tokens with no real value. They reset when mainnet launches.

**Q: Can I lose money?**  
A: Impossible. Unlimited test tokens. No real money involved.

**Q: Will I earn real Pi for mainnet?**  
A: No, but demonstrating usage helps prove the model for mainnet launch.

**Q: How long does checkout take?**  
A: ~2 seconds typically. Sometimes slower if SAIB enforcer is busy.

**Q: Can I cancel an order?**  
A: Yes, within 1 hour. After that, depends on the service (delivery = in transit).

**Q: What if SAIB is offline?**  
A: System falls back to standard transactions. Your purchase still processes.

**Q: How do I refer friends?**  
A: Each pillar has a "Refer" section with your unique code. Share the link!

**Q: Can I withdraw testnet tokens?**  
A: No, they only exist in testnet. Mainnet will require real Pi network participation.

**Q: Which pillar makes the most sense to start?**  
A: **pi-Dex** (fastest), then **Deliveries** (most tangible), then **Rentals** (best long-term).
